Ethics & Limits

Responsible
AI forecasting.

Data Quality Matters

Biased or incorrect financial data could mislead business decisions. Garbage in, garbage out — always verify your inputs.

Augment, Don't Replace

SmartProfit should support — not fully replace — human financial experts who bring contextual judgment to decisions.

Transparent Predictions

Users should understand how predictions are made. SmartProfit shows its inputs, factors, and reasoning clearly.

Why 100% accuracy is impossible

  • Unpredictable market changes
  • Inflation and interest rate shifts
  • Unexpected events like economic crises
  • Incomplete or messy financial data
  • Human behavior and sentiment shifts

SmartProfit follows the 1-second rule: if a financial analyst could estimate profit quickly from past data, a regression model can learn it reliably.
